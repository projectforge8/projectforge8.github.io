About Project Forge

Forge is an internal product opportunity research and experimentation project. Its planned tools help organize marketplace and product information, assess potential ecommerce opportunities, and document results. It is currently in private development and is not offered as a public service.

Marketplace integrations are subject to the relevant platform's approval and access requirements. Project Forge is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Etsy or other marketplaces.

Privacy

This informational website has no account registration, payment forms, analytics scripts, or advertising cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical information as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Questions posted through GitHub are public. Please do not post passwords, API keys, personal details, or other sensitive information. Any future application that handles marketplace account data will need its own appropriate privacy information before public use.
